## About

**Route AI**, built by **UniRoute AI**, helps students find the right university, scholarship, and visa path for studying abroad — matched to them by AI, not generic search results.

Choosing where to study abroad usually means digging through scattered, outdated, and conflicting information spread across hundreds of university websites, forums, and agents. Route AI replaces that with one place: verified admission deadlines, real program details, and personalized matching, backed by an AI assistant that actually answers your questions with sources.

## What we're building

- 🎓 **University & program matching** — tailored to a student's grades, budget, country preferences, and goals
- 📅 **Verified admission deadlines** — checked directly against official university sources, kept current
- 💰 **Scholarship & funding guidance** — find funding options that fit a student's profile
- 🛂 **Visa path guidance** — country-specific guidance for international students
- 💬 **Conversational AI assistant** — ask in plain language, get sourced, grounded answers
- 📊 **Program comparisons** — tuition, fees, intake dates, and requirements side by side

## How it works

1. Tell Route AI your academic background, budget, and goals
2. Get matched with universities and programs that actually fit
3. See verified deadlines, costs, and requirements — no guesswork
4. Ask follow-up questions anytime, in natural language
