**Route AI** helps students find the right university, scholarship, and visa path for studying abroad — matched to them by AI, not generic search results.

We built Route AI because choosing where to study abroad means digging through scattered, outdated, and conflicting information across hundreds of university websites. Route AI brings verified admission deadlines, program details, and personalized matching into one AI assistant.

## What we're building

- 🎓 **University & program matching** — tailored to a student's grades, budget, and goals
- 📅 **Verified admission deadlines** — pulled and checked against official university sources
- 💰 **Scholarship & funding guidance**
- 🛂 **Visa path guidance** for international students
- 💬 **Conversational AI assistant** — ask questions in plain language, get sourced answers
